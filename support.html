Life Focus Support

A private devotional journal that brings your whole walk with God into one place, and shows you how it connects.

Contact us

Email lifefocusapp@gmail.com, or open the menu (☰) → Contact Support in the app. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I start my Morning Journey?
Open Life Focus and tap Begin. You'll be asked if you dreamed, then guided through putting on the Armor of God, prayer, worship, today's Bible reading, and a Call to Remembrance.

How do I record a dream?
On the Did You Dream? page, tap Yes, I Dreamed. You can link the dream to biblical symbols, Scripture, and the people in it.

How do I write sermon notes?
Open the menu (☰) → Sermons and tap +. Use the bar above the keyboard for headings, bold, colors, lists, photos, and videos. Type a Scripture reference like John 3:16 and tap it (or rest the pointer on it on iPad or Mac) to read the verse.

How do I add a video to a sermon?
In a sermon, tap the picture-with-a-+ button and choose Video from Photos, Video from Files, or YouTube Link. Videos play right inside the sermon.

Where do the biblical symbol meanings come from?
Symbol meanings come from Understanding the Dreams You Dream by Ira L. Milligan, used with permission. You can also add your own symbols and meanings.

How does my journal get to my other devices?
Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Life Focus on each iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Your records sync on their own while the app is open. The first time on a new device can take several minutes.

How do I back up my journal?
Your journal is kept in your private iCloud and included in your device's iCloud Backup. You can also open the menu (☰) → Backup Life Focus to save a complete copy.

How do I send a sermon or prayer to my group?
Tap Send with Ministry Messenger on a sermon, prayer request, or testimony. Ministry Messenger is our free companion messaging app.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, or open the menu (☰) → Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and your journal stays yours.

Is my information private?
Yes. It stays on your devices and in your private iCloud account. We can't see it. Read the privacy policy.
