How to use Life Focus

How do I start my Morning Journey?
Open Life Focus and tap BEGIN. You'll be asked if you dreamed, then guided through putting on the Armor of God, prayer, worship, prayer requests, petitions, Father Speaks, today's Bible reading, and a Call to Remembrance. You can also jump to any step from the menu (☰) under Life Focus.

How do I record a dream?
On the Did You Dream? page, tap Yes, I Dreamed. You can link the dream to biblical symbols, Scripture, and the people in it. If you didn't dream, tap Continue Journey.

Where do I find my journal entries later?
Open the menu (☰). Under Databases you'll find everything you've saved: Appointments, Bible Study, Biblical Symbols, Dreams, Father Speaks, People, Prayers / Requests, Petitions, Prophecies, Sermons, and Testimonies.

How do I write sermon notes?
Open the menu (☰), choose Sermons, and tap +. Use the bar above the keyboard for headings, bold, colors, lists, photos, and videos. Type a Scripture reference like John 3:16 and tap it to read the verse.

How do I add a video to a sermon?
In a sermon, tap the picture-with-a-+ button and choose Video from Photos, Video from Files, or YouTube Link. Videos play right inside the sermon.

How do I keep track of the people I pray for?
Open the menu (☰) and choose People. Add each person once; then, as you record dreams, prayer requests, and other entries, you can link the people they're about.

How do I send a sermon or prayer to my group?
Tap Send with Ministry Messenger on a sermon, prayer request, or testimony. Ministry Messenger is our free companion messaging app; it opens with the text ready to send.

Where do the biblical symbol meanings come from?
Symbol meanings come from Understanding the Dreams You Dream by Ira L. Milligan, used with permission. You can also add your own symbols and meanings.

Will my journal be on my iPad and Mac too?
Yes. Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Life Focus on each iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Your records sync on their own while the app is open. The first time on a new device can take several minutes.

Is my journal backed up?
Yes. Your journal is kept in your private iCloud, so it comes back when you sign in on a new device, and it's included in your device's iCloud Backup.

Is Life Focus free for young people?
Yes. If you're under 18, Life Focus is free until you turn 19. The app checks your age range with Apple during setup; no subscription is needed.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Open the menu (☰) and choose Subscription, or go to Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and your journal stays yours.

Is my information private?
Yes. Your journal stays on your devices and in your private iCloud account. We can't see it. Read the privacy policy.

How do I get help?
Open the menu (☰) and choose Contact Support, or email lifefocusapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
