Life Focus Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Life Focus is made by Generations Arising. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the Life Focus app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

The short version

Your journal belongs to you. We do not collect, see, sell, or share any of the information you enter. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What you store in the app

You can record dreams and their interpretations, prayers and prayer requests, petitions, prophecies, testimonies, "Father Speaks" entries, sermon notes, Bible notes and highlights, appointments, and the people in your life. This information is stored:

  • On your device, and
  • In your own private iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, so your journal stays in sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Information in your private iCloud database is protected by Apple and is only available to devices signed in to your Apple Account. We, the developer, cannot access it. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple protects iCloud data.

Contacts

If you choose to import contacts, Life Focus reads the contacts you allow so you can add them as people in your journal. They are stored only on your device and in your private iCloud. Life Focus never uploads your contacts anywhere else.

Photos and videos

If you add a photo or video (for example, to a sermon or a person), the app only receives the items you pick; it can't see the rest of your library. They are stored with your records on your device and in your private iCloud.

Voice recordings and dictation

If you record a prayer or journal entry, the recording is stored with your records. If you ask Life Focus to turn a recording into text, it uses Apple's speech recognition, which may send the audio to Apple to process it. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

Worship music

If you connect Apple Music, Life Focus reads your playlists so it can play your chosen worship songs during the Morning Journey. Your music library is not sent to us.

YouTube videos in sermons

If you add a YouTube link to a sermon, the app downloads that video's picture from YouTube and plays the video in YouTube's own player. When you do, YouTube (Google) may receive information such as your device's internet address, under Google's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to YouTube unless you add or play a YouTube video.

Age range

When you set up Life Focus, the app may ask Apple for your age range (not your birth date) to decide which plan applies. We do not store or receive your age.

Sharing

When you use Share, Print, or Send with Ministry Messenger, the text you chose is passed only to the app or person you pick. Ministry Messenger is our separate companion app and has its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else. It contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting. Features that find connections between your records run entirely on your device.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never receive your card details. The app only checks with the App Store whether your subscription is active.

Backups you create

If you use Backup Life Focus, the app creates a file containing your journal and saves it wherever you choose. You control that file. Anyone you share it with can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Deleting your information

You can delete any record in the app. Deleting the app removes its records from that device. To remove the copy in iCloud, delete the records in the app before removing it, or manage iCloud storage in your device's Settings.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Everything a user enters stays on their own devices and in their own iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
